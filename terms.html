Terms of
use.

These are draft terms for the Sovera fashion-design prototype. They need review by the site owner and qualified legal counsel before the website accepts real accounts or public user content.

Current status: the public GitHub Pages site is a frontend prototype. Real user authentication, public cloud posting, AI try-on and account moderation are not active until the backend is configured and tested. Preview designs are stored in the browser and are not a secure cloud vault.

1. The service

Sovera provides fashion concept exploration tools: stylised 3D garment visualisations, sketch-style views, material and colour choices, measurements, inspiration examples and planned community features. Visualisations, measurements and cost estimates are exploratory and may not represent a manufacturable garment, precise fit, physical fabric behaviour or production-ready pattern.

2. Your designs and content

You retain rights you already own in your original designs and uploaded content. Do not upload or publish designs, photographs, logos, patterns or other materials unless you have the rights and consent needed to use them. The app should only display a separate public preview when you explicitly choose to publish it; it should not publish the full source design payload or complete private measurements.

Before real community publishing is enabled, the site owner should define the limited licence required to store, render, moderate and display the preview you choose to publish. A public preview may be copied, photographed or recreated by viewers; Sovera cannot guarantee that all copying or screenshots are prevented.

3. Accounts and security

Preview profiles are not real accounts. When live account features are enabled, users must provide accurate account details, keep their authentication credentials confidential and report suspected account compromise. The owner must activate email verification, rate limits, CAPTCHA/bot controls, secure storage policies and tested row-level security before inviting public users.

4. Community behaviour

Do not post copied work without permission, harassment, threats, spam, unlawful content, private personal information or content that violates another person's rights. The planned community includes reporting and moderation controls. Moderators may hide comments or remove a public preview, and administrators may restrict accounts under a published moderation policy. A formal appeals process and abuse response contact should be added before public launch.

5. Photos and AI previews

AI outfit editing, when enabled, requires an explicit user action and consent. The generated image can change details of the garment or photograph and must not be relied on for garment sizing, tailoring, product conformity or body measurements. Review the Privacy & data choices notice before uploading a personal photo.

AI provider usage may incur a charge to the site owner. The owner must set spending controls, request limits, monitoring and abuse safeguards before making the feature available.

6. Unacceptable use and limits

Do not attempt to access another user's private designs or files, bypass access controls, probe administrator functions, interfere with the service or use it to distribute malicious content. This experimental service is supplied without a guarantee that visualisations will be error-free, always available or suitable for production. Local demos can be removed by clearing browser data; live cloud features require separately configured retention and deletion rules.

7. Changes and legal review

These draft terms do not identify a legal entity, jurisdiction, contact address, final dispute process or production retention schedule. The site owner must fill in these details and review applicable consumer, intellectual-property, privacy, data-protection and online-content obligations before public launch. Nothing here guarantees legal compliance or replaces tailored legal advice.

8. Contact

Until a dedicated support contact is established, project feedback can be sent through the Sovera GitHub repository. Do not share passwords, API keys, account tokens or private user photos in public issues.
